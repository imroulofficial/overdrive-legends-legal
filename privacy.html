Privacy Policy

Overdrive Legends · Red Pen Studio · Effective date: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Overdrive Legends mobile game ("the Game") and the third-party services built into it collect, how that information is used, and the choices you have. The Game is published by Red Pen Studio ("we", "us", "our").

1. Summary

  • The Game is offline-first. Your progress, garage, coins and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not offer accounts or cloud saves.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Google services inside the Game (AdMob, Firebase and Google Play Billing) collect some device and usage data, as described below.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change that choice at any time in Settings.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your race progress, unlocked cars and upgrades, coin balance, purchase entitlements and settings in the Game's private storage on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted if you uninstall the Game or clear its data, and it may be included in your device's own Android backup if you have enabled that feature with Google.

3. Information collected by third-party services

3.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

The Game shows rewarded ads (which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward) and interstitial ads (full-screen ads shown between races). Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where permitted, personalise them, AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • IP address, from which an approximate (city or region level) location may be inferred;
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type and app version;
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, tapped or completed.

Personalised ads use this data to show ads based on your interests. Non-personalised ads still use some of this data for frequency capping, fraud prevention and aggregated measurement, but are not based on your past behaviour.

3.2 Consent management (Google User Messaging Platform)

We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage privacy choices:

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR): when you first launch the Game, a consent form explains how data is used for ads. Ads are personalised only if you consent. If you decline, you will see non-personalised or limited ads.
  • United States (CCPA/CPRA and other state privacy laws): where required, the Game shows a message that lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. When you opt out, we signal restricted data processing to Google.

You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the Game. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

3.3 Firebase Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the Game is played in aggregate, for example which tracks are raced, where players stop progressing, and how often features are used. Firebase Analytics collects an app instance ID, device and app information, approximate location derived from IP address, and in-game events (such as "race finished" or "car upgraded"). This information is not linked to your name or any account, and we use it only in aggregated reports. Analytics data is retained for 14 months and then deleted.

3.4 Firebase Crashlytics

If the Game crashes or hits a serious error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report containing a stack trace, the device model, operating system version, app version, available memory and disk space, and a random installation identifier (Crashlytics installation UUID). We use these reports only to find and fix bugs. Crash reports are retained for 90 days.

3.5 Firebase Remote Config

Firebase Remote Config lets us adjust game settings (for example event dates, reward amounts or difficulty tuning) without releasing an update. To deliver the right configuration, it uses the Firebase installation ID and basic device and app information. It does not collect gameplay content.

3.6 Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card, bank details or billing address. When you make a purchase, the Game receives a purchase token, order ID and product ID from Google Play so it can deliver your item and restore permanent purchases. Your purchase is governed by the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. How we use information

  • To run the Game and deliver items you buy or earn;
  • To show ads that fund the free version of the Game, and to deliver rewards for rewarded ads;
  • To diagnose crashes and improve performance and stability;
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the Game is played so we can improve it;
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud.

Our legal bases under the GDPR are: your consent (personalised ads and, where required, analytics); our legitimate interest in keeping the Game stable, secure and funded (crash reporting, non-personalised ads, fraud prevention); and performance of a contract (delivering in-app purchases).

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information for money. Data collected by the services above is processed by Google LLC and its affiliates as described in their policies. Under some US state laws, allowing personalised advertising may be considered "sharing" or a "sale"; you can opt out as described in section 3.2. We may also disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger or acquisition of Red Pen Studio, in which case this policy will continue to apply.

Google's policies:

6. International transfers

Google may process data in the United States and other countries outside your own. Google relies on approved transfer mechanisms such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Analytics data is kept for 14 months and crash reports for 90 days, after which they are deleted automatically. Because we do not operate accounts, we cannot identify you in our analytics or crash data from your name or email. If you would like us to delete analytics data linked to your device, contact us (section 11) and we will explain how to find your app instance ID in the Game so we can process the request.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority. California and other US state residents may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, to opt out of sale or sharing for targeted advertising, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. To use any of these rights, contact us at the address in section 11.

9. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has used the Game, contact us and we will help remove any associated data. Players under the age of majority where they live should use the Game and make purchases only with permission from a parent or guardian.

10. Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit using TLS. Data stored on your device is kept in the Game's private app storage, which other apps cannot read. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use reasonable measures to protect information.
